Welcome to Student Academic Services

Overview and Instructions

Welcome to the academic enrollment system. Through this portal, you can explore the current semester course listings, inspect prerequisites and syllabi details, and submit your formal enrollment selections.

Please review the Course Catalog prior to submitting your application to verify schedule compatibilities and seat availability. Once you have selected your target courses, proceed directly to the Course Registration Form.

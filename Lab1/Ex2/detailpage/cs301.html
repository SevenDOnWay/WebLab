CS301: Data Structures & Algorithms

Course Overview

Rigorous study of fundamental data organizations and algorithm design paradigms. Topics include asymptotic analysis (Big-O), linear collections (stacks, queues, lists), trees, balanced binary search trees, hash tables, heaps, and graph algorithms.

Course Specifications

  • Credits: 4 Credit Hours
  • Instructor: Dr. Ada Lovelace (alovelace@university.edu)
  • Lecture Schedule: Tuesday & Thursday, 11:00 AM – 12:30 PM
  • Prerequisites: CS101 and Discrete Mathematics with a grade of C or better.
  • Enrollment Capacity: 30 total seats (22 available).

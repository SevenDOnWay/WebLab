CS101: Introduction to Computer Science

Course Overview

An introduction to the foundational concepts of computing, algorithmic thinking, and computational problem solving. Students develop program solutions in Python, learning variables, control flow, functions, and elementary data structures.

Course Specifications

  • Credits: 4 Credit Hours
  • Instructor: Dr. Alan Turing (aturing@university.edu)
  • Lecture Schedule: Monday & Wednesday, 09:00 AM – 10:30 AM
  • Prerequisites: High school algebra or equivalent math proficiency.
  • Enrollment Capacity: 40 total seats (35 available).
